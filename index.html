Who We Are

Built from the Factory Floor

VE Three D Solutions is a Malaysian-based 3D printing enterprise specialising in custom industrial parts. We work with factories, machine operators, and production teams who need precise, durable components — fast.

Our background is in CNC and industrial automation, which means we understand what parts actually need to do in a production environment. Every print is designed with real factory conditions in mind, not just aesthetics.

We handle the full process: from your drawing or idea, through prototype confirmation, to the finished printed part ready for deployment.
